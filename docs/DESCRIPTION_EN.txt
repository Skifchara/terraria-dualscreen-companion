TERRADUO COMPANION  -  version 1.0.2
Second-screen companion for Terraria on dual-screen Android devices (built and tested on the AYN Thor)
=====================================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
Terraria runs on the top screen as usual. The bottom screen becomes a live, touch-controlled panel
that reads the real game state (no OCR, no fake data, no virtual taps) and changes the game only
through the game's own routines, so the game stays the authority.

Everything is drawn from the game's own sprites, fonts and backgrounds taken from YOUR installed copy
of the game (see "Legal" below) and the interface is available in English and Russian.

FEATURES
  * Inventory: all 40 slots, coins, ammo, trash slot, sort. Drag items between slots: move, swap,
    stack. Double-tap a stack to split it in half into the first free slot.
  * Item window: big sprite, name in rarity colour, damage / speed / defence / tool power /
    healing, the game's own tooltip lines and the reforge (prefix) percentages in green and red.
    Buttons: Use, Equip, Favourite, Split 1, Split half, Drop, Delete, Put in chest, Take.
  * Edge gestures while dragging an item:  top = drop,  bottom = use / equip / summon,
    right = trash slot,  left = favourite.
  * Equipment: armour, accessories, vanity, dyes, pets, mounts, light pets, hooks and their dyes.
  * Crafting laid out like the game: list of craftable items, details, ingredients with how many
    you have, hold the Craft button to repeat.
  * Chests and banks (chest, piggy bank, safe, defender's forge, void vault): the window opens by
    itself with Loot all, Deposit all, Quick stack, Restock, Sort and Close.
  * Hotbar: tap to select, selected slot follows the game.
  * Health and mana as the game's ornate bars; tap them to flip to hearts and stars (golden hearts
    for life above 400, animated).
  * Buffs and debuffs with their icons and remaining time. Info items (watch, depth meter,
    compass, weather radio) show their readings in the top bar.
  * Map: smooth panning, pinch or button zoom, follow-the-player, town NPC heads with names.
  * Background that follows where you are: the game's own parallax backdrops for your biome and
    part of the world, day / night tint, clouds, rain and snow, a soft vignette and white edge
    flashes when lightning strikes.
  * Screen-edge flashes when you lose health (red), heal (green) or use mana (blue).
  * Hide HUD: removes the game's own HUD from the top screen so the whole world is visible.
    While it is hidden the controller works for the companion: L1 / R1 change the hotbar slot,
    Y cycles the tabs (Inventory > Equipment > Craft > Map > ...).
  * English / Russian (button in the top bar; follows the game language by default).

WHAT YOU NEED
  * A dual-screen Android device (made for AYN Thor; the bottom screen is Android display 4).
  * Terraria for Android bought on Google Play (package com.and.games505.TerrariaPaid).
  * TL Pro (com.pixelcurves.terlauncher) - the mod loader the companion talks to. Single-player only.
  * A PC with Python 3.10+ and adb, once, to prepare the sprites from your own copy of the game.

WHAT IS IN THIS PACKAGE
  Terraduo-Companion-1.0.2.apk        the companion app (signed with a development key)
  Terraduo-Companion-mod-v6.tl      the TL Pro mod pack that exposes the game state
  icon.png                          the app icon (original artwork)
  tools/                            PC scripts that decode sprites and backgrounds from your game
  screenshots/                      every screen of the app
  DESCRIPTION_EN/RU.txt, GUIDE_EN/RU.txt   this text and the user guide
  source/                           full source code (inside the full archive)

LEGAL / SAFETY
  * No game assets are included. Sprites, fonts and backgrounds are decoded on YOUR PC from YOUR
    installed copy and copied to the app's private folder on your device. Do not redistribute them.
  * Nothing here removes or bypasses licence checks or protection. Do not install cracked or
    re-signed copies of Terraria. The game APK is never modified.
  * The mod only changes game state through the game's own functions (moving items, crafting,
    using items). Back up your saves before trying any mod; this one never touches save files
    directly, but the game does when you play.
  * Single-player only. Do not use with online servers.

KNOWN LIMITS
  * NPC conversations are shown as text; buying, selling and the dialogue buttons are not
    implemented yet.
  * Underground backgrounds depend on the game reporting them; some biomes may fall back to a
    generated backdrop. Some background pages are missing from certain game builds.
  * Chest, trash slot, double-tap split and the controller shortcuts were written against the
    game's code and need a restart of the game through TL Pro after the mod is updated.
  * Built for 1.4.x of the Android port of Terraria; other versions may differ.
